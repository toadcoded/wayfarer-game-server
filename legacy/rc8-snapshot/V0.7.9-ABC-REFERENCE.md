No real-browser/GPU or physical-device visual check was completed. ABC fields remain fixed to the lab rig, affect cosmetic cloth only, and are not replicated. Character-follow behavior, authoritative character lift and collision-aware cloth are not implemented. v0.8 remains an upcoming integration milestone.
