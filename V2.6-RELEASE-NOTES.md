# Wayfarer v2.6.0 — hostile encounters and field combat

Wayfarer v2.6.0 extends the complete v2.5 multiplayer realm. The server remains authoritative for player movement, combat actions, projectile flight and impact ticks, enemy health/aggro/respawn, drop rolls, and loot collection.

## New hostile regions

- **Old Bell Grave:** three skeletons; each has a 5% chance to drop a steel warhammer.
- **Mirewake Hollow:** three zombies; each has a 5% chance to drop a steel battleaxe.
- **Broken Standard Camp:** six thugs, bandits, and muggers in a multi-combat area; thugs can drop steel daggers, bandits steel swords, and muggers steel rapiers, each at 5% per kill.
- At the campsite, nearby enemies can join an alerted fight. Use spacing, lure a single target away, retreat, or keep melee-protection prayer active. Enemies leash back to their camp and respawn.

## Combat controls and loops

- Melee offers **slash, crush, strike, and lunge**; weapon compatibility, reach, damage, and cooldown are enforced by the server.
- Ranged attacks consume arrows and magic spells consume runes. Arrows and spells travel as authoritative projectiles; damage is applied at the scheduled impact tick, and a target that disappears before impact is a miss.
- Rare steel drops appear as ground loot and must be collected into the server-owned inventory.

## Movement and presentation

- Travel modes are **walk 2.25 m/s**, **jog 4.4 m/s**, and **run 6.8 m/s**. Older three-field input remains at its established 4 m/s pace for compatibility.
- Character rigs and the canvas fallback are slightly smaller; animation follows the new travel modes.
- Low-poly 3D and 2D fallback views show the new hostiles, their attack/projectile cues, and weapon-shaped steel drops. Highland ridges and color variation are richer, and terrain/building surfaces have thicker visual relief without changing authoritative navigation.
- The encounter HUD calls out multi-combat danger, melee style, ranged/magic controls, threat status, and the exact campsite steel loot.

## Release and limits

The package version is **2.6.0**. `DEPLOYMENT-V2.6.md` documents supported Node versions, build/test commands, public-host configuration, and profile-save durability limits. The public sandbox deployment is temporary; it is not an always-on hosting commitment. No manual GPU/device visual review is claimed by these release notes.
