# v1.2 art inspiration layers

The v1.2 expansion translates the supplied reference images into original procedural game motifs. No supplied reference image is shipped as a world texture or copied as a scene.

## Palette anchors

Visual sampling of the references reinforced five restrained game palettes rather than a single rainbow wash:

- **Sunward coast:** sea cyan / leaf green / warm fruit orange / flower gold
- **Moonlight cavern:** near-black stone / deep teal / cyan crystal / amber lantern
- **Codex archive:** charcoal stone / desaturated blue-green / parchment gold
- **Astral forest:** midnight blue / cool periwinkle / mushroom coral
- **Celestial machinery:** near-black navy / violet / magenta / cyan-blue

## Gameplay readability rules

1. Cyan and violet identify magic/celestial interactables; amber identifies safe human-made light and craft landmarks.
2. Gatherable silhouettes stay chunkier and lower-frequency than decorative foliage.
3. The PolyCodex is readable from a distance by its dodecahedral core, twelve-node halo and moving orbit rings.
4. Cavern navigation retains bright timber/water/crystal edges against dark stone so decoration does not obscure walkable space.
5. Continuous animation is cosmetic and pauses or reduces under the existing renderer/reduced-motion boundaries.
6. Reference imagery inspires color, rhythm and motif only; it never changes authoritative collision, rewards or combat outcomes.
