## Coverage highlights

The full suite includes strict resonance/codex validation, authoritative tick-coupled casting and effects, XP/inventory invariants, WebSocket client join/input/snapshot/disconnect/rejoin against a local realm, host origin/Host/cookie safeguards, SQLite persistence and migration, configurable guest-enrollment throttling, graceful shutdown, and process-kill/restart recovery. The compatibility manifest remains at simulation revision 16 / scene revision 3 / 50 ms ticks.

The initial full run exposed three stale test assumptions, not gameplay-authority failures. They were corrected before the successful rerun:

1. The Babylon bundle test now recognizes either the dependency-free fallback bridge or the canonical source-generated bundle.
2. The browser-client stub DOM now implements the `dataset`, `classList`, attributes, append and style APIs used by the real v1.2 interface, and asserts the twelve PolyCodex nodes are created.
3. The release-host test now checks the v1.2 page banner instead of the obsolete v1.0 banner.

The authoritative server behavior was not weakened to make tests pass.

## Guest-enrollment throttling

The demo's “Too many new guest profiles” response was traced to the per-address five-new-profiles-per-minute limiter, not the total profile cap; eight of 50 demo slots were in use. `GUEST_REGISTRATIONS_PER_MINUTE` is now configurable from 1–100, defaults to 5, and is documented for Docker deployments. Existing signed guest cookies bypass new-profile throttling. The demo is configured at 20 new profiles per address per minute while retaining its 50-profile total cap. An integration test verifies the `429`/`Retry-After` behavior and cookie reuse.

## Remaining limits

- These are automated source/build tests, not a physical iPhone/Android/GPU visual review or a live production browser session.
- The Docker/Caddy stack, public DNS/TLS, long-duration uptime and Internet-scale load were not exercised here.
- No Render service or other paid persistent host was provisioned. No permanent public endpoint or production launch is claimed.
- The project remains a single persistent small realm with guest identities; account recovery, multi-device accounts, sharded authority and every planned MMO profession/system are not implemented.
