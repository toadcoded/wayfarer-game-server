# rc.25 · HUD comfort, light and shallow wading

The in-game Settings tab adds Small, Normal and Large panel sizes, a 35–80% viewport maximum height slider and switches to show/hide the graphics and world/conversation overlays. Each overlay has a close button. Close the game panel with its close button or Escape, then reopen with Open game panels; connection and movement controls remain accessible. Tab navigation includes Settings. Controls retain at least 44px touch targets, with 48px game buttons and 52px buttons in Large mode. Layout dimensions change directly rather than scaling the canvas or pointer coordinates. Validated device-local preferences retain the chosen tab, size, height, collapsed state and overlay visibility. Existing v1 preferences remain readable.

The renderer adds a gradual cosmetic twenty-minute day/night cycle, a changing sky gradient, stars that fade in daylight, a warm sun-like disc/cool moon appearance, corresponding directional/ambient light and water highlights. Real WebGL uses one 1024px character shadow map with at most 96 player mesh casters; Balanced disables it. Water uses local standard-material specular highlights, not screen-space or mirror reflections. No new remote shaders, reflection passes, texture generation packages or frameworks are required. Weather and reduced-motion climate pause retain their existing bounded behavior.

Shared realm navigation now permits grounded wading to a maximum depth of 0.45 metres across the whole character footprint. Deep water, steep slopes, obstacles and invalid ground remain blocked; movement never grants swimming, floating or a client-selected water height. Real shallows exist near x57/z−14 in the current causeway. Navigation semantics changed, so simulation revision is now 15 and scene revision 2. Profile schema stays 5. Older contract clients/journals require explicit migration rather than silently replaying under changed navigation. The contract manifest is regenerated from current constants.

This is a focused iteration on the current playable realm. It does not finish every proposed visual feature or turn the existing map into a full MMO. Browser/phone visual and GPU performance review remains open. A reduced-motion preference pauses atmospheric time; movement and authoritative simulation continue as before.
